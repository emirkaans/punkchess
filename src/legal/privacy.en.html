Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can play chess on punkchess.com without creating an account, or sign in to use our online features. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. It also serves as our privacy notice under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Data controller

Emir Kaan Sarıçam is responsible for the processing of personal data. For privacy questions or requests under KVKK, contact info@punkchess.com.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in using Google, we receive:

  • Your Google account ID, so we can recognise your account when you return.
  • Your email address, to identify your account and contact you if necessary.
  • Your Google profile name, used only to suggest a username. We do not store it.

We cannot access your Google contacts, files or other account content. Your email address is not visible to other users.

Information we keep

  • Account information: Your username, account creation date and selected jacket.
  • Sessions: A randomly generated session key identifies your signed-in browser. We store an irreversible hash of the key rather than the key itself. Sessions expire after 60 days.
  • Games and ratings: Your ratings across game categories, online match records, moves, player names, results, dates and earned patches.
  • Puzzles: Your puzzle attempts, puzzle rating, daily puzzle results and Puzzle Rush personal best.
  • Social features: Players you follow and who follow you, your gang, gang wars, tournament results, match invitations you send and receive, your chosen spray tag and, if you joined through an invite link, the user who invited you.
  • Moderation records: If an account is blocked for breaking the rules, the date and reason are kept. Actions taken by site staff (which staff member did what, to which account) are logged for security.
  • Technical information: Your IP address may appear in one-minute request counters used to prevent abuse and in server logs retained for a few days to investigate technical problems. Error details such as the page URL, browser type and error message may also be recorded. Error reports without IP addresses are retained for 14 days.
  • Website analytics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page visits. It does not use cookies or personally identify visitors.
  • Information stored in your browser: Settings such as theme, pieces, sound and language, locally played games, nickname, guest puzzle rating and streak, and a random player ID are saved in your browser's local storage. This information is not sent to our servers.

What other players can see

Your public profile may display your username, join date, ratings, online games, patches, selected jacket and spray tag, your gang, your followers and the players you follow, and how many friends you invited. Your username and rating may also appear on leaderboards, tournament standings and gang war results. Online match records and games shared through a link can be viewed by people who have access to that link.

Why we process your data

  • Creating and maintaining your account, profile, ratings and games: Necessary to provide the service (KVKK Article 5/2-c).
  • Protecting the site, preventing abuse and cheating, and investigating technical issues: Our legitimate interests (KVKK Article 5/2-f).
  • Meeting legal obligations when required by law (KVKK Articles 5/2-a and 5/2-ç).

We do not use your personal information for advertising, marketing or user profiling.

Cookies

We only use cookies necessary for signing in securely and maintaining your session. These include a session cookie and temporary cookies used during the sign-in process. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

If you arrive through a friend's invite link, the inviter's username is kept in a cookie for 30 days. If you create an account within that time, the inviter is recorded on your account; the inviter only sees how many people they invited.

Where data is stored and shared

We do not sell, rent or share personal information for advertising purposes. punkchess uses Cloudflare, Inc. infrastructure. Our database is located in Europe, while website content is delivered through Cloudflare servers around the world. This means personal data may be transferred internationally. Such transfers are carried out subject to the requirements of KVKK Article 9. When signing in with Google, only the account information described above is exchanged with Google LLC.

Emails you send to info@punkchess.com are forwarded through Cloudflare to Google's email service (Gmail); our replies are sent through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France). These emails are used only to answer your request.

How long we keep information

  • Account information, games and ratings are retained while your account remains active.
  • Security request counters expire after one minute, and server logs are deleted within a few days.
  • Database backups are retained for up to two months. Cloudflare's recovery history extends up to 30 days. Deleted account information is removed from backups once these retention periods expire.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account whenever you want. Click your username in the top-right corner and select "Delete account". Your account, email address, sessions, ratings, patches and puzzle results will be deleted.

Previously played online matches remain in the game history because they are also part of your opponents' records. Your username may remain visible in those records, but it will no longer link to your deleted profile. Your follows, gang membership and match invitations are deleted with your account. Records of actions taken by site staff are kept for security.

Your privacy rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to find out whether your personal data is being processed, request information about its processing, learn why it is used, and find out who it has been shared with. You may also request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, or deletion where the legal conditions are met.

You may object to decisions made solely through automated processing that negatively affect you, and seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You can send requests to info@punkchess.com. We respond within the legal period of 30 days, generally free of charge. You may also have the right to complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

Children's privacy

You must be over 13 to create an account. Playing without an account is available to everyone.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the changes here and revise the date above. Significant changes will also be announced on the website.
